Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what information Bentam Chalk™ (operated by Damiksh Enterprises, registered at 1037/8 AVC, Bodla, Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India) collects when you use our website, and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Order details — your name, phone number, delivery address, and locality, collected at checkout to fulfil and deliver your order.
  • Delivery pincode — used only to check whether we deliver to your area.
  • Location (optional) — if you tap "Use my current location," your device's GPS coordinates are used once, in your browser, to confirm you're within our Agra delivery area and suggest your address. We do not track or store your location continuously.
  • Payment information — we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details. All payments are handled directly and securely by Razorpay; we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order
  • To create and update your delivery tracking schedule
  • To contact you about your order via WhatsApp, phone, or email

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Protect Your Information

  • Staff (delivery team, owner) accounts are protected with hashed passwords and secure login sessions — no one can view raw passwords, including us.
  • Your order and delivery data is only visible to you (via your private tracking link), our delivery team (for their assigned deliveries), and the business owner.
  • Your delivery tracking link uses a long, random, unguessable code — it is not indexed by search engines and is not shared publicly.

Cookies

Our website uses a minimal session cookie only for staff (delivery team/owner) logins, to keep them signed in securely. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies on the customer-facing site.

Your Rights

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.
